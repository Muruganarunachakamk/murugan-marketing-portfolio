About

How I think about marketing

I'm an MBA Marketing candidate at Amrita School of Business, working at the intersection of consumer research and business strategy. My academic work centers on understanding how advertising media and digital touchpoints shape consumer decision-making — most recently through a primary survey study on advertising media effects, and current research on consumer trust in AI-based customer support.

My analytical foundation was built during a B.Com in Accounting & Finance and internships in audit and financial reconciliation, which grounded my approach to research in rigor and evidence rather than assumption. I bring that same discipline to marketing problems: segmenting audiences, testing hypotheses, and translating data into positioning and communication decisions.

Alongside coursework, I represent my institution through the Public Relations Committee, coordinating outreach and event promotion across student and external stakeholder groups — building the communication and relationship-management skills that sit alongside analytical capability in marketing work.
